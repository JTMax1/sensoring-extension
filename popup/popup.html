How it works

Bleepr monitors video subtitles and closed captions in real-time. It calculates sub-second word timings, instantly mutes video volume, and synthesizes a TV censor beep the moment explicit words are uttered.

Tip: Ensure Closed Captions (CC) are enabled on the video player for Bleepr to detect speech!
